Grandma Bettie charged two dollars and threw in the coffee.

Bettie Delgado cut hair in her upstairs kitchen at 214 Alder for 38 years. Half the street sat in that red chair at some point, usually with a kid on their lap waiting for a turn.

Her granddaughter Rosa opened the shop downstairs in 2016. The chair came down with her. So did a few of Bettie's rules, which are still taped next to the till.

  • If you don't need color, we'll tell you.
  • Fringe trims are free between appointments. Just walk in.
  • Running late? Text us and we'll shuffle things around.
  • Want a quiet cut? Tick the box when you book. We'll keep the chat to a minimum.

The price board

Every price includes a wash and finish unless it says otherwise. If your hair needs extra time, we tell you the price before we start, never after.

    Before you come in

    The small stuff people usually ask us on the phone.

    Color? Patch test first

    New to color with us, or it's been six months? Pop in for a five minute patch test at least 48 hours before. It's free.

    Cancelling

    Give us 24 hours and there's no charge. With less notice we ask for half the price, because someone else could have had that chair.

    Parking

    Two-hour street parking on Alder. The lot behind Kowalski's Bakery is free after 5pm and all day Saturday.

    Getting in

    Step-free from the street. The back chair lowers all the way down, and the basin tilts forward if leaning back hurts your neck.

    Paying

    Card, cash or tap. Tips are kind and never expected. Gift cards are behind the counter.

    Little ones

    Kids sit in the window chair. It has a booster seat and a box of very old comics.
